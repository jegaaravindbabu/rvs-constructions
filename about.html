A builder you can call by name.

RVS Constructions was founded by Dev Kumar in 2013, from a small office in Kodungaiyur, North Chennai. Since then the team has completed more than sixty new buildings: independent houses and villas, commercial spaces and steel-structure warehouses, along with interiors and renovations.

The company has grown, but the way it works has not. One team handles design, approvals, construction and finishing, so you deal with the same people from the first site visit to the day you get the keys.

How we work.

The same five steps on every project, whether it is a villa or a warehouse.

  1. 1

    Site visit and consultation

    We walk your plot with you, understand how your family lives, and talk through budget and timelines.

  2. 2

    Design and approvals

    Plans, elevations and 3D views, followed by the paperwork for building approvals.

  3. 3

    A clear estimate

    An itemised quote with the materials named, so you know exactly what you are paying for.

  4. 4

    Construction with updates

    Foundation to finishes, with regular photo updates and site visits whenever you like.

  5. 5

    Handover and after care

    A walkthrough of every room, the keys, and a team that still picks up the phone after you move in.
